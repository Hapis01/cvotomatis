Deskripsi pekerjaan IT Business Analyst PT Hermes Solusi Integrasi

Requirements
Willing to be placed at Pulomas Office, Jakarta.
Bachelor's degree from a reputable university (IT, Computer Science, or related field)
Minimum 3 years' experience as Business Analyst or similar role
Strong understanding of business analysis processes (gather needs, document, and validation solutions)
Experienced in handling incidents and analyzing issues
Familiar with ticketing tools (Freshdesk, Azure DevOps, etc.)
Able to do root cause analysis and write clear documentation
Understand testing processes (SIT/UAT)
Able to create and review test scenarios and results with QA/Tester
Familiar with Agile/Scrum and working with cross teams (PM, PO, Dev, QA)
Good analytical thinking and problem-solving skills
Strong communication and documentation skills
Job description
- Handle service requests and resolve incidents
- Analyze issues and support problem resolution
- Communicate progress and updates to users
- Coordinate with internal/external teams
- Create and maintain documentation & knowledge base
- Support daily operational activities
- Assist in testing (SIT/UAT) and review testing results if required
- Provide on-site support when needed
